COOKING SCIENCE

What heat and water do to food.

Cooking changes texture, water content, digestibility, and some vitamins. The effect depends on the food and the method.

Choose ingredients ↗
PROTEINDenatures

Heat unfolds protein structure; it does not usually make protein vanish.

WATERMoves or evaporates

Braising retains a broth; roasting removes water and can concentrate nutrients per 100 g.

VITAMINSMethod-dependent

Vitamin C and some B vitamins are water- and heat-sensitive; short steaming often retains more than prolonged boiling.
