THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE CALCULATOR

How food becomes a number.

A plain-language guide to the physiology and food chemistry behind this educational model.

Try the calculator ↗
PROTEINAmino acids

Digestion breaks dietary proteins into amino acids and small peptides used to build and repair tissues.

CARBOHYDRATEGlucose + fiber

Digestible starches become glucose; fiber resists digestion and is partly fermented by gut microbes.

FATFatty acids

Bile emulsifies fat and enzymes release fatty acids for absorption and cell membranes.

What each calculator value means

Calories

Energy estimated from food composition. Protein and carbohydrate provide about 4 kcal/g; fat about 9 kcal/g. The app uses USDA food records where available.

Vitamins & minerals

Micronutrients act as enzyme helpers, antioxidants, structural minerals, or signaling molecules. A number indicates quantity in the source record, not a guarantee of absorption.

Cooking and structure

Heat unfolds protein structure (denaturation), softens plant cell walls, and can destroy or release compounds. Water loss can concentrate nutrients per 100 g.

Absorption

The intestine absorbs nutrients through transporters and digestion products. Fiber, meal composition, microbiota, and individual physiology all change bioavailability.

How our educational score is formed

  1. Sum the selected foods' per-100 g nutrient records.
  2. Compare food-family variety with broad plate targets.
  3. Apply a clearly labeled cooking-method adjustment for heat, water, and added fat.
  4. Show missing categories and the source/data status beside the result.

The score is a teaching aid, not a nutrient adequacy test, disease-risk estimate, or individualized recommendation. Nutrigenomic interaction indices in the research demo are hypotheses and are not clinical measurements.
